Does chiropractic actually work?

Yes. Chiropractic is evidence-based and recommended by the American College of Physicians as a first-line treatment for back pain — before drugs or surgery. Our 500+ five-star reviews speak for themselves.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans, including auto injury (PIP) and workers' compensation. We'll verify your benefits before your first visit so there are no surprises.

Does an adjustment hurt?

Not at all. Most patients describe it as a feeling of relief and release. You may hear a popping sound (that's just gas releasing from the joint), but the adjustment itself is gentle and controlled.

How many visits will I need?

Every patient is different. After your exam, we'll create a personalized plan. Some patients feel better after one visit; others benefit from a course of care. We'll never push unnecessary visits.
