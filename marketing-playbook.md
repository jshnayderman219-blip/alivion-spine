# PART 14: CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

## Critical Optimization Points

1. **Phone answer rate:** Every missed call = lost patient. Target: 95%+ answer rate during business hours. Use call forwarding to cell if front desk is busy.

2. **Booking friction:** Reduce clicks. Calendar link should be 1 click from any ad. Phone number should be tappable on mobile.

3. **Show rate:** Text reminders at 24hr + 2hr before appointment. Include: "Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule."

4. **No-show recovery:** Auto-text within 30 min of missed appointment: "We missed you today! Still want relief? Reply YES and we'll reschedule this week with no penalty."

5. **Treatment plan acceptance:** Use a printed report of findings. Show the patient their X-ray/MRI. Explain in plain language. Offer payment plans. "Most patients start with [Plan A]. We also have [Plan B]."
