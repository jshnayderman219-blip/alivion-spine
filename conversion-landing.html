Absolutely. Chiropractic and spinal decompression are evidence-based, recommended by the American College of Physicians as first-line treatments for back pain — before drugs or surgery. Our 500+ five-star reviews speak for themselves.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major insurance plans including auto injury (PIP) and workers' compensation. We verify your benefits before your first visit so there are zero surprises.

Does it hurt?

Not at all. Most patients describe the feeling as relief. Spinal decompression is gentle and comfortable — many patients even fall asleep during treatment.

Every patient is unique. After your exam and X-rays, we create a personalized plan. Some feel better after visit one; others benefit from a structured course. We never push unnecessary visits.

Many of our patients came to us after being told surgery was their only option. Non-surgical spinal decompression has an 85%+ success rate for appropriate candidates. Come in for a free MRI review and we'll tell you honestly if we can help.
